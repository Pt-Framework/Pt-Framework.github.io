Detailed Description

A Form attaches one content Control and is the point where the content hierarchy joins a window or another form host. It delegates measuring and layout to that content, forwards view requests, and coordinates focus traversal, shortcuts, and mnemonics for all attached controls. Use setContent() to replace the content; the form does not own the control, so the caller must keep it alive until it is detached.
